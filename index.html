how we work

01

intake call

we start with the full picture: your product, your team, your culture and values and where this hire needs to take you. a job description is the starting point, not the brief.

02

market check

before sourcing begins, we validate expected skillset, seniority, compensation and timeline against the real market. if something doesn't add up, we tell you here — not six weeks in, just to get you "on the hook".

03

sourcing

the engineers worth hiring aren't browsing job boards. we reach people who are well-employed and not looking — based on their fit, not availability.

at the risk of being immodest: we get replied.

04

assessment

every candidate is thoroughly interviewed before you see them. what lands in your inbox is a brief with our judgment, context and flags — not a cv dump.

05

reporting

you're posted weekly on: where the search stands, what's moving, what the market is telling us, where we're adjusting, what's next. including when the news is hard. no silence.

06

close

we're in the room through negotiations until the offer is signed. 3-month replacement guarantee if the hire doesn't work out.
